## II. The 417 reading — an auditor's error, marked, not vanished

The first pass of this audit returned **417** for ABRAHADABRA and flagged the edition's 418
as a failure. Re-weighing letter by letter showed the fault was in the audit's own input:
the string fed to the instrument (אבראהדאברא) had **ten** letters — the Aleph after He had
been dropped in transcription. The word has eleven letters; the complete spelling
אבראהאדאברא weighs **418**, as printed. The false reading is preserved here because that is
what it was: an error of the auditor, caught by the audit's own discipline, and the reason
no verdict in this program is ever taken from a single pass.
