## Elements and their reasoning
- **♊ The Twins (the frame):** two pillars (x=696, x=1304) under two crossbars — the Gemini glyph itself, enlarged into architecture. Reasoning: his Sun is Gemini (3°14′); the Twins are the container of the device, as on the coin.
- **♉ The Bull (the heart):** circle r=222 centered (1000, 1036), horns rising from its shoulders and piercing the top crossbar, tips pointed. Reasoning: the Taurus glyph is a horned circle; his Moon, Mercury, Venus, and Mars all stand in Taurus — the bull is not decoration, it is the majority of the chart.
- **The core:** a dark-moon crescent cradling a pale Sun dot. Reasoning: at the first breath the Moon stood 12.1° short of the Sun, 23½ hours from the New Moon — the crescent is the balsamic Moon; the dot is Sol in Gemini.
- **The rulers (Variant A only):** gold medallions riveted on the pillars — ♀ Venus (ruler of Taurus) on the left pillar, ☿ Mercury (ruler of Gemini) on the right, glyphs knocked out in indigo. As on the coin.
- **The rim (Variant A only):** double ring carrying **28 ticks** (the days of the 13/28 moon) and **13 nodes** (the 13 moons; the 13th Gate). His framework numbers, counted exactly.
- **Palette:** gold #d4af37 / pale gold #f0d98c on deep indigo #141033 — the house colors of the coin and the covers.
